What Atlas does

One system. Three things it stops costing you.

Atlas reads the sales and purchase records you already keep. It catches customers who have quietly stopped ordering, records every receipt against the right account without anyone retyping it, and answers questions about your own numbers in plain English.

You find out in days, not at quarter close.

A customer who has ordered every fourteen days for a year, now on day nineteen, is a problem you can still fix. The same customer three months later is a problem you have lost. Atlas holds a separate baseline for every account and checks all of them every morning.

Ranked by what is at stake

Every quiet account with its last order, its own average cycle, and how many cycles it has missed — biggest number first.

With the reason and the message

Why they slipped, what to offer, and a draft you can send as it is — so the call takes two minutes, not twenty.

Delivered where your team already is

Slack or WhatsApp, every week. Nobody has to remember to open a dashboard.

Nothing goes missing between the receipt and the ledger.

Data entry is where money quietly goes wrong — a receipt somebody forgot to upload, an amount keyed twice, a supplier bill that never made it in. Atlas closes the gap between someone holding a receipt and it being recorded correctly.

Read, matched, checked

Supplier, amount, date and bank reference pulled out; the right account chosen; checked against what you have already recorded so a duplicate is caught before it is booked.

Approved in one click
