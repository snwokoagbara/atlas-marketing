About

Built for the businesses the software industry skipped.

Atlas is made for FMCG manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers and pharmacy groups — companies with hundreds of repeat customers, real revenue, and none of the analyst headcount that enterprise software quietly assumes you have.

Why it exists

A distributor with three hundred customers cannot watch three hundred reorder patterns by hand. So they watch the total, and the total looks fine right up until it does not.

The information needed to catch it early is already sitting in their sales records. It has always been there. What was missing was something that would look at it every day and say a name out loud.

The same turned out to be true of the books. The problem was never that people could not do the data entry — it was that a receipt has to survive the trip from somebody’s pocket into the system, and often it does not.

Who it is for

FMCG manufacturing and distribution, wholesale, and pharmacy groups. Businesses where the same customers buy again and again, and where a quiet month is the first sign of a lost account.

Where it runs

Nigeria first — Lagos, Port Harcourt and Abuja — because that is where the problem was put in front of us, and because WhatsApp is where this market actually works.

How it is built

On the records you already keep. No new system of record, no migration, no stock-count habit to adopt. If it needs you to change how you work, it has failed.
